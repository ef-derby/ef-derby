## 🔥 Features

* **📸 Match OCR** - Instant stat extraction from post-match screenshots.
* **📊 Season Analytics** - Win rates, tactical radar, and performance ratios.
* **🧠 Playstyle Personas** - AI-classified tactical archetypes.
* **⚡ Rivalry Tracking** - Head-to-head records against specific opponents.
* **🔑 100% Safe & Private** - Add your Google AI Studio key for unlimited extractions.

---

## 🔑 Unlimited Extractions (Google AI Studio Key)

Derby includes a built-in daily extraction quota for all users. To unlock **unlimited extractions with zero rate limits**:

1. Get a free API key from [**Google AI Studio**](https://aistudio.google.com/app/apikey).
2. Open **Derby** on your phone → Navigate to **Settings**.
3. Tap **Gemini API Key**, paste your key, and tap **Save**.

> 💡 *Google AI Studio provides a free tier that is 100% free with no credit card required.*

## 📱 Download & Installation

1. Go to the [**Latest Releases Page**](https://github.com/ef-derby/ef-derby/releases/latest).
2. Download the latest `derby-vX.X.X.apk` to your Android device.
3. Tap the downloaded file to install.
   > **Note**: If prompted, allow *"Install from unknown sources"* for your browser or file manager.
4. Launch **Derby**, configure your username, and upload your first match screenshot!

## 🛡️ Privacy & Safety

* **No Game Login Required**: Derby **never** asks for your Konami ID, password, or login credentials.
* **Safe & Non-Intrusive**: Matches are processed purely via image recognition from screenshots.
* **Full Data Ownership**: Delete individual match records or reset your data anytime from App Settings.
